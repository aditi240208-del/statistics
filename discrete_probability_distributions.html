Poisson Distribution

The Poisson distribution models the number of events occurring independently in a fixed interval when events happen at a constant average rate.

1. Input Parameters
λ represents the average number of events in a fixed interval of time, space, or another defined unit. Zero is allowed.
Selected value

PMF formula
P(X = k) = e−λ λk / k!

Geometric Distribution

The Geometric distribution models the trial number on which the first success occurs in independent trials with a constant probability of success.

1. Input Parameters
p is the probability of success on each independent trial and remains constant from trial to trial. Enter 0 < p ≤ 1.
Selected value
p = 0.35
PMF formula
P(X = k) = (1−p)k−1 × p,   k = 1,2,...
2. Geometric Distribution Statistics
Mean · 1/p
2.8571
Variance · (1−p)/p²
5.3061
Standard deviation
2.3035
Second moment · E[X²]
13.4694
Skewness
2.0126

Convention: X is the trial number on which the first success occurs, starting at X = 1. Skewness is undefined at p = 1 because the variance is zero.

3. PMF Calculator
Formula
P(X = k) = (1−p)k−1 × p

4. CDF Calculator

P(X ≤ k) means the probability that the first success occurs on or before trial k.

P(X > k) is the probability of getting no successes in the first k trials.
